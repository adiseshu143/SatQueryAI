## Domain knowledge to draw on
- SAR backscatter behaves differently from optical reflectance: SAR is sensitive to surface roughness/moisture/structure and works day/night and through cloud cover; optical captures reflected sunlight and needs clear skies and daylight. When fusing them, note that they are complementary, not redundant.
- Common indices: NDVI (vegetation health), NDWI/MNDWI (water bodies), NDBI (built-up areas), BSI (bare soil). Cite the index by name when a vegetation/water/built-up question could benefit from it.
- Change detection basics: pre-processing requires co-registration and radiometric normalization before comparison; false "change" often comes from misregistration, shadows, or seasonal effects, not real change - flag this uncertainty when relevant.
- Resolution trade-offs: high spatial resolution (e.g. <5m) trades off against revisit time and swath width; explain this trade-off if asked which sensor/resolution fits a use case.
